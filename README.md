# Southeast Asia Economic Analysis & Forecasting

## Overview

This project analyzes the economic development of Southeast Asian countries using historical GDP, GDP per capita, and population data. The study applies data visualization, clustering techniques, and forecasting methods to identify economic development patterns and predict future economic trends across the region.

## Objectives

* Analyze long-term economic growth trends in Southeast Asia.
* Compare economic performance among countries.
* Identify groups of countries with similar development characteristics.
* Forecast future economic indicators and growth trends.
* Provide insights into regional economic development.

## Dataset

The dataset includes economic indicators of Southeast Asian countries, such as:

* Gross Domestic Product (GDP)
* GDP per Capita
* Population
* Historical Economic Data

The data covers multiple Southeast Asian countries over several decades, providing a comprehensive view of regional economic development.
